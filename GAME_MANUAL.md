# POKÉDEX — Game Manual

A real Pokédex. Point it at a Pokémon, press SCAN, and it identifies what
it's looking at, tells you about it, and records the catch forever.

Nine regions. 1025 Pokémon. 72 gym badges.

---

## How catching works

There's no random encounter, no wild grass. **You find the Pokémon in the real
world.** A card, a figure, a plush, a picture on a screen, a poster. If a
camera can see it and Google can recognise it, you can catch it.

1. **Main Menu → Reverse Image Version → Play Catch 'Em All**
2. Aim using the live feed on screen
3. Press **PAGELEFT** to scan
4. The screen freezes on the exact photo it's checking
5. On a hit: full Pokédex entry, spoken name, catch recorded, XP awarded

Press **BACK** once to return to the camera, twice to leave.

If it says "Pokemon not found", the photo wasn't clear enough or the image
search didn't recognise it. Better light, less glare, fill more of the frame.

## XP and levels

| Event | XP |
|---|---|
| Catch any Pokémon | 100 |
| **First time** catching that species | **+400 bonus** |

So a brand new species is worth **500**, a repeat is worth **100**.

Level N costs `500 × N` XP:

| Level | XP for that level | Total XP |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 500 | 500 |
| 3 | 1,000 | 1,500 |
| 4 | 1,500 | 3,000 |
| 5 | 2,000 | 5,000 |
| 10 | 4,500 | 22,500 |
| 50 (max) | 24,500 | 612,500 |

**Chasing new species levels you 5× faster than re-scanning the same Charizard.**

## Unlocking a region's badges

What locks isn't the region — it's the **badges**.

> **Catch 60% of a region to unlock its 8 gym badges.**

Until then, that region's badges stay grey no matter what you catch. Once you
cross the line, every badge you already qualify for lands at once.

## Strategy

**Start with Kalos.** 44 Pokémon unlocks 8 badges — barely half of what Kanto
demands. Alola (53) and Galar (58) are next easiest.

**Chase the species badges early.** Pikachu, Charizard, Lucario, Mimikyu —
these are the easiest Pokémon in the world to find merchandise of. Each one is
a badge sitting there waiting for the region to unlock.

**New species over repeats.** 500 XP vs 100.

**The "complete the dex" badges are the real wall.** Nine of the 72 need a
full regional Pokédex. Kalos at 72 is the only one that's remotely realistic.

**Watch the yellow line.** Trainer → Regions shows every region's bar with the
unlock point marked. That's your map.

## Trainers

Create a profile from **Trainer Status**: name, city, age, gender, and a team
— **Red**, **Blue**, or **Yellow**. Your team colours the UI.

Multiple trainers can share the device. Each keeps their own catches, XP,
badges and favourites, completely separate. Switch from
**Trainer → Info → Switch / New Trainer**. In the trainer list, **TAB**
deletes (with a confirmation).

---

## Data

**Main Menu → Data**

- **Erase my catches** — wipes the current trainer's catches, XP, badges and
  favourites. Profile survives.
- **Erase EVERYTHING** — every trainer, every catch. Factory reset.

Both ask first. Neither can be undone.

Everything saves automatically and survives a reboot.

## A note on the badge art

The badges are drawn by the app as original geometric shapes — real names,
real gym leaders, real colours, but not Nintendo's artwork.

If you want different art, drop PNGs into `~/pokedexv5/badges/` named after
the badge (`boulder_badge.png`, `cascade_badge.png`, `melemele_trial.png`).
The app uses your file if it's there and falls back to the drawn shape if it
isn't — you don't need to supply all 72.
