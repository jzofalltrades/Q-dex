Drop your own badge art here as PNG and the app will use it instead of the
drawn shape.

Filename = badge name, lowercase, spaces as underscores:
  boulder_badge.png
  cascade_badge.png
  rising_badge.png
  melemele_trial.png
  champion_stamp.png

Square images work best (they get scaled to fit). Transparent background.
Anything missing just falls back to the drawn shape - you don't need all 72.
